How we started.

A small technical partner, founded by an engineer and a doctor.

It started with a conversation, not a business plan.

A doctor we trust kept describing the same frustrations. Hours lost each week to copy-paste data entry. Faxed forms. Reminder phone calls. Software that hasn't meaningfully improved in a decade.

The tools sold to practices are expensive platforms built for hospital systems. Nothing helps with the twenty small things that actually eat the day. As an engineer, I kept thinking the same thing: these are solvable problems.

Healix is what came out of that. A small technical partner that builds the simple fixes. Automations. Basic AI assistants. Working websites. The things that let our clients spend their time on the work they actually trained for.

The first fixes were small. A reminder that sent itself. A form that stopped living on paper. An inbox that sorted itself before anyone opened it. These small changes gave hours back every week. That has been the pattern ever since: find the boring thing done by hand, and quietly take it off the list.

We stay small on purpose. We build it, we maintain it, and we explain it in plain language. No platform login, no lock-in, no selling you something you don't need. When the work is done, you own it.

The two of us.

Why healthcare first, but not only healthcare.

Healthcare is where we saw the problem first, through a doctor's day. But the problem isn't only a doctor's. Academics, researchers, and small professional offices are stuck in the same manual work. If it's repetitive and boring, the fix usually looks the same.
